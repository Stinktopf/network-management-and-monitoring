# Preparation for future course offerings

The current materials reflect the schedule for winter semester 2026/27.
Before using them in a subsequent semester:

- Move **How we work together**, **Your project, your decisions** and
  **The scope of course projects** into the course introduction, before topic
  selection and the first project work. Introduce workshop procedures,
  independent group work and project requirements at that point.
- Place the corresponding workshop and project information on the Moodle
  course page before project work begins. Avoid duplicating the introductory
  slides later in the course.
- Update semester labels, dates, deadlines, session lengths and presentation
  slots. Check assessment information against the requirements for that offering.
- Rebuild the HTML, PDF and PowerPoint exports and check that Moodle and the
  course materials agree.

Keep the current slide sequence for the ongoing winter semester 2026/27.
