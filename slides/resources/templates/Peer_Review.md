# In-class peer review — 22 January 2027

Assigned paper / team:

Reviewer name:

Read the assigned paper during class. Write one page **legibly by hand**, in **German or English**. Work independently and submit your own review.

## 1. Summary

State the research question, approach and main result in your own words.

## 2. Method

Assess one methodological choice using evidence from the paper. Consider the baseline, measurement, data treatment or reproducibility.

## 3. Judgment

Identify a strength and a limitation. Explain how each affects the conclusion.

## 4. Revision

Propose one feasible improvement. Say what should change and why it matters.

## Hand in during class

Give the handwritten page to the instructor on **22 January 2027, before leaving**. Include your name and the reviewed paper. The instructor keeps the original, makes a copy and gives it directly to the reviewed team during the session.

## Optional bonus

One qualifying review can improve its individual author's passing examination grade by **one grade step**. The best possible grade is **1.0**. Failing grades remain unchanged. The review does not determine the other team's grade. Shared team reviews do not qualify for the bonus.
