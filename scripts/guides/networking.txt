Inspect: peer AS and State in BGP, OSPF neighbor state, then the exact customer
prefix, active route, next hop and outgoing interface in the forwarding table.
Decision: if a peer is down, inspect its transport first. If the route exists in
BGP but is not active for forwarding, investigate selection/next-hop resolution.
If the active route exists on both ReefNet devices, continue to the customer and
the return path in step 4. Record which observation supports each hop of the path.
@@ solution
ReefNet is AS65000, the customer AS65010, Lagoon AS65100 and Pacific AS65200.
Edge 01 learns the customer prefix through eBGP and shares it internally through
iBGP. OSPF provides reachability inside ReefNet, including the internal path
between Edge 01 and Edge 02. A BGP session alone does not prove service delivery.
